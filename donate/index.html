School Supply Drive

The 2026 drive put $4,000 of supplies into 13 schools

Collection boxes at partner businesses, libraries, and schools, restaurant fundraiser nights, and a GoFundMe raised more than $500 in cash, which we spent on supplies in bulk. Community and partner donations brought the total to over $4,000 in supplies, packed into kits and delivered to 13 Title I schools across Delaware in August 2026.

Money given to the drive goes to three things: the supplies, the collection boxes, and getting the kits to schools.

  • $4,000+ in supplies
  • 13 Title I schools
  • 200+ students reached

Where donations go

Nobody on the team is paid

SkillSphere is run by student volunteers. There is no office and no staff, so donations go to the programs.

  • School supplies bought in bulk for Title I schools in Delaware. The 2026 drive: $4,000+ across 13 schools.
  • Materials for free workshops in robotics, marketing, and financial literacy. Six programs so far.
  • Printing and distribution of the BPA study materials, so chapters without a budget get the same set.
  • Chapter expansion into Pennsylvania. Read the pitch deck or the 2025 to 2026 Impact Report.

SkillSphere Foundation is a Delaware nonprofit corporation recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 39-2560980. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Bylaws, policies, and the board are on the transparency page.
