Where Donations Go

SkillSphere is student-led, volunteer-run, and focused on direct impact. Donations help us keep our work accessible and free for students.

Purchasing school supplies for students and Title I schools across Delaware.
Supporting free student-led workshops in marketing, STEM, robotics, and professional readiness.
Covering materials, outreach, and basic logistics needed to run community programs.
Helping expand SkillSphere chapters so more students can lead programs at their own schools.
