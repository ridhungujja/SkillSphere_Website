Programs

What we run, and what it costs students

Nothing. Every workshop, every study guide, and every supply kit is free. This page explains the three things SkillSphere does and where each one stands.

01

Workshops

Hands-on sessions run by our student team, online and at partner sites, for elementary, middle, and high school students. Six so far.

Marketing Foundations (October 18, 2025) ran over Zoom: students learned ethos, logos, and pathos, then designed a product flyer in Canva and pitched it to the group. The robotics workshop (December 7, 2025) at Code Ninjas Pike Creek had middle school students building and programming driving robots with LEGO SPIKE kits. Financial literacy is the third track.

  • 6 workshops held
  • 200+ students reached
  • Code Ninjas Pike Creek host partner
  • Free to every student

02

School Supply Drive

Collection boxes at partner businesses, public libraries, and schools across Delaware and Pennsylvania, restaurant fundraiser nights at Chipotle Middletown and Tropical Smoothie Café Elkton, a GoFundMe, and bulk purchasing with the money raised. Kits go to students at Title I schools.

The 2026 drive raised more than $500 in cash, $420 of it through GoFundMe, and with community and partner donations produced over $4,000 in supplies for 13 Title I schools, distributed through the Delaware Regional Dream Center. In August the team handed 45 kits to students in person on the Dover Middle School campus, with Delaware Secretary of Education Cindy Marten at the table.

  • $4,000+ in supplies collected
  • 13 Title I schools
  • Dream Center distribution partner
  • Free to every student

03

BPA study materials

Free preparation resources for Business Professionals of America competitors, written by students who compete. Well-funded chapters buy prep materials; most cannot. We are writing the set so every competitor has the same starting point. The materials are an official Delaware BPA preparation resource, endorsed by the Delaware BPA State Officer Team, and we are working with BPA's National Secondary President on wider CTSO use.

The curriculum is eight modules, built alongside Delaware BPA Open Events. Each module has a study guide, a 20 to 25 slide instructional deck, three practice tests with answer keys, examples, and review resources. It is in progress, with modules released as they are finished. FBLA and TSA versions are planned next.

  • 8 modules planned
  • 3 practice tests per module
  • Delaware BPA official prep resource
  • Free to every student
