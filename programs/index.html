Programs

What we run, and what it costs students

Nothing. Every workshop, every study guide, and every supply kit is free. This page explains the three things SkillSphere does and where each one stands.

01

Workshops

Hands-on sessions run by our student team at partner sites. Six so far.

At Code Ninjas Pike Creek, students built and programmed driving robots with LEGO SPIKE kits. In the Marketing Foundations session they learned ethos, logos, and pathos, then designed a product flyer in Canva and pitched it. Financial literacy is the third track.

  • 6 workshops held
  • 200+ students reached
  • Code Ninjas Pike Creek host partner

02

School Supply Drive

Collection boxes at partner businesses, restaurant fundraiser nights at Chipotle Middletown and Tropical Smoothie Cafe Elkton, and bulk purchasing with the money raised.

The 2026 drive ended with kits delivered to students at the Dover Middle School campus in August, with distribution coordinated through the Delaware Regional Dream Center. Delaware Secretary of Education Cindy Marten joined the team for the handoff.

  • $4,000+ in supplies collected
  • 13 Title I schools
  • Dream Center distribution partner
Fund the next drive

03

BPA study materials

Free preparation resources for Business Professionals of America competitors, written by students who compete. Well-funded chapters buy prep materials; most cannot. We are writing the set so every competitor has the same starting point.

The curriculum is eight modules, built alongside Delaware BPA Open Events. Each module has a study guide, a 20 to 25 slide instructional deck, and three practice tests with answer keys. It is in progress, with modules released as they are finished. FBLA and TSA versions are planned next.

  • 8 modules planned
  • 3 practice tests per module
  • Delaware BPA curriculum partner
