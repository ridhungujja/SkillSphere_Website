What we have been doing

Workshops, the supply drive, and press coverage, most recent first.

August 24, 2026

Supplies delivered in Dover, with the Secretary of Education

SkillSphere delivered packed supply kits to students at the Dover Middle School campus. Delaware Secretary of Education Cindy Marten and Capital School District leadership joined the team at the table. The kits represent over $4,000 in supplies collected across the summer and went to students at Title I schools.

WBOC-TV and WMDT 47abc both covered the distribution.
