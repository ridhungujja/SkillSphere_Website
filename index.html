Our Story

Started by Students. Built for Everyone.

SkillSphere started from a simple observation: the skills that matter most in life — leadership, communication, professional presence — are rarely taught in school. Three students, Ridhun Gujja, Devkrishna Manappattu, and Yashas Chittimalla, built a nonprofit run entirely by students, for students.

Since then, SkillSphere has grown to a team of 17, run multiple workshops across STEM, marketing, and leadership, launched a statewide school supply drive across Delaware, and begun building a chapter model so students at any school can bring this experience to their own community.

Ridhun Gujja, Devkrishna Manappattu, and Yashas Chittimalla find that business and technology skills are not being taught in their classrooms. They decide to build something to fix that.

Where We Operate

Rooted in Delaware. Built for Everywhere.

SkillSphere is currently based in Delaware. Our workshops, supply drive, and partnerships are all rooted here — in real schools, real businesses, and real communities.

But from the start, we've been building with scale in mind. Our curriculum, workshop framework, and chapter model are all designed to travel. A student at any school, in any state, should be able to start a SkillSphere chapter and run with it.

Our Initiatives

Beyond the Workshop

SkillSphere doesn't stop at skill-building. We show up for our community in real, tangible ways.

School Supply Drive

A biannual initiative collecting and distributing essential school supplies to students in under-resourced communities across Delaware — through a network of school, business, and community partners.

Restaurant Fundraisers

One-day community fundraiser events where local restaurant partners donate a percentage of sales to support our programs and supply drives. A win for the community and for students.

Chapter Expansion

Community Partnerships

We collaborate with schools, businesses, faith organizations, and community centers to amplify our reach and create more opportunities for students across Delaware and beyond.

Currently Active

School Supply Drive 2025

Right now we're running our first biannual school supply drive across the state of Delaware. The goal is simple — collect essential school supplies and get them into the hands of students who need them most.

We're doing this through donation boxes at local businesses and schools, restaurant fundraiser nights, and direct monetary donations that let us purchase supplies in bulk. Everything we collect goes directly to Title I schools across Delaware.
